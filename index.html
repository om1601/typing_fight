TYPEFIGHTER

Type fast. Fight faster.

How To Play

  • Just type. The glowing word on screen is your only control — no mouse, no arrow keys.
  • Correct letters turn green, wrong letters turn red. Backspace fixes mistakes.
  • Speed = power. Fast, clean words trigger kicks, uppercuts and roundhouses. Slow words land a weak punch — or nothing, if you miss.
  • Build combos. Chain correct words in a row for damage multipliers, up to an Ultimate Attack at 30.
  • Watch the enemy. It attacks automatically — type fast enough while it winds up and you'll interrupt the hit.
  • Survive & win. Drop the enemy's HP to zero before yours hits zero.
